Our Story & Botanical Sanctuary

Brew & Bloom was born at the intersection of slow botanical living and ethical, altitude-grown coffee. We partner directly with regenerative shade-canopy farms across Oaxaca, Huila, and Sidama, hand-roasting in micro-lots every Tuesday and Thursday morning.

Inside our 1920s Portland timber warehouse, living green walls and seasonal flora filter the morning sun while antique copper lever espresso machines extract single-origin sweetness. A tranquil respite carved directly into the city.
